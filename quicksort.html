❓ HOW TO CHOOSE PIVOT ELEMENT?

🟧 First or Last Element: Simple to code, but makes the algorithm very slow O(n^2) if the data is already sorted.

🟫 Random Element: Safest and most popular for real-world use because it prevents worst-case slowdowns.

🟦 Middle Element: Picks the exact center index of the array.

🟪 Median-of-Three: Looks at the first, middle, and last elements, then picks the middle value among them for the best balance.

Once chosen, the pivot is moved to its correct final position, and the process repeats for the left and right sides.
